Nano

Wi-Fi — move the Nano to another network it knows

Away from home the Nano joins the hotspot by itself; this is for when both networks are in range. After it moves, put this iPad on the same Wi-Fi and reopen the app — https://gadgetboy-nano.local:8766/ works everywhere.

This iPad

Start-up, in order

  1. Away from home: phone hotspot on first, keep that screen open.
  2. Power the Nano. Wait for the status line at the bottom to go green — about 90 s. Nothing works before that.
  3. This iPad on the same Wi-Fi as the Nano; open PhraseKit. Red status line = different Wi-Fi.
  4. Tap Test sound here once. No sound: volume up, Silent Mode off, close and reopen the app.
  5. Then choose the languages and start. If the iPad has slept, reopen the app before the next patient.
